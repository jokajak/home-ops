# Household TELOS

> The context engine's source material. This is the part of PAI worth keeping: capture who we
> are and what we're trying to do, then let the platform continuously reconcile current state
> against ideal state.
>
> **Status:** 🟡 Skeleton — to be filled by Josh. Prompts below are provocations, not answers.

PAI's TELOS is single-principal (one person). The interesting question here: **does the
household have its own TELOS**, or is it a federation of individual TELOSes? Probably both —
a shared household layer + per-person layers. We'll model that as we go.
